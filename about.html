Meet Manzar alam

Adding ease to sergeon's hand.

Manzar is an reg- pahrmacist, merketing incharge and first line entrepreneur from Gaya ji. She was taught life drawing, graphic design and fashion illustration at the Central Saint Martins, London College of Fashion and The Royal Drawing School in London. She also have a Master of Science in Business and Economics from Lund University.

Helena mainly works with brands within the fashion, lifestyle and hospitality industries. Craftsmanship and art are close to heart and influence all steps of her creative process. She normally creates everything by hand using traditional mediums such as graphite pencils, ink and watercolour. Designs that need to be printed or published online are digitised and edited in the Adobe Creative Suite.

Helena lives in Saltsjöbaden in Stockholm with her husband, daughter and twin boys.
